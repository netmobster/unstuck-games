CONTACT

Say something.

It goes to one person. That person reads everything, answers most things, and is slower than he would like to admit.

Reach out if

✓Something broke, or a game did something so strange it deserves a witness.
✓You want to playtest early and tell the truth about it.
✓You know a game that died and deserved better. Especially if nobody remembers it.
✓You write, draw or make noise, and you think your work belongs in one of these.
✓You want to say hello. That is a whole reason.

Don't reach out if

✗You want to know whether the ferret is single. She is unavailable and uninterested.
✗You have a great new microtransaction idea. We have read it. It was about gems.
✗You would like to hop on a quick call about growth, funnels or user acquisition.
✗Your proposal contains the words battle pass, season pass or engagement loop.
✗You are offering to buy the domain. It cost twelve dollars and it is not for sale.
✗You are an SEO expert who has noticed some issues with our website.

No list, no tracking, no newsletter signup hidden in this button. If you want the newsletter, that lives on the game pages and is its own decision.
