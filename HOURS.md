# Jay-Hours Ledger

The production thesis measures **human attention per shipped game** — not CPU time, not
elapsed time, not lines of code. If CC grinds 50,000 simulations overnight, that's free.
If Jay spends 45 minutes explaining why it isn't fun, that's 45 minutes.

CC fills this in; Jay corrects the numbers. Estimates are marked `~`.

## Game #1 — Orbis

> **Caveat:** Orbis is a port of Jay's *own existing game*. The hours Jay spent designing and
> building the original are not in this ledger, and a port of your own game is the easy case —
> the fun was already found and proven. This measures the Unstuck *rebuild* cost, not a new game.
> **Game #2 — rescued from someone else's corpse — is the real test of the production thesis.**
