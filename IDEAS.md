| 2026-09-20 | **SQUINT — a standalone Unstuck game first, a SEREN feature second** | A two-player wagering game in which **no card is ever played**: three each, and the whole game is public betting about what you hold. Built 30 Aug in `SEREN/labs/squint` as a *test fixture*, not a minigame - **558 hands of soak, rankings enumerated (two inversions against Brag, counted not inherited), the honesty control verified, and zero hands actually played.** ⭐ **Reframed 2026-09-20 (Jay): ship it as its own UG game before it is anything to do with SEREN.** That changes what blocks it, and mostly it unblocks it. **Moot now:** the prediction test - it asks whether an NPC playing this reads as *that NPC*, which is a SEREN question and not a question about two humans betting; and `OPEN-QUESTIONS` A3/A4 (*is the opponent human or NPC*, *fixture or product*) are answered by the reframe itself. ⛔ **Promoted to the only blocker: the trust model.** The commit-reveal is not safe player-vs-player as built - whoever makes the nonce can grind it offline for a favourable deal. Both sides must contribute entropy. Cheap, but it must be decided and written down or the honesty guarantee is decoration. ⚠️ **And three things that were quiet as a fixture get loud as a product:** free-text stakes between strangers is an open text field two people bet on and nobody has thought about moderation · **a dropped socket is not a character act** - single-player, walking away had a forfeit price and that was the point; online, people close tabs · spectators have no role in the fog model at all, and a spectator who sees both hands is a channel to a player. ⭐ **It also fits the house:** the payoff is **what you found out about someone**, not a score (`unstuck-not-about-winning`), and the engine is already written and soaked, so the work is a browser and a second player rather than a design. **It would be the studio's first two-player game** - every other one is you and a system. ⛔ Non-negotiables carry over whole: the server owns the deal, there is no `full_state()`, sealed events never reach a client, and **on a `see` that loses, the winner never shows - permanently**, which is the entire mechanism. ⭐ **And the shape it wants, Jay 2026-09-20: live, two-player, and possibly the studio's first MOBILE game.** It is unusually well suited to a phone and that is not a coincidence - **three cards, a handful of buttons, no twitch input, and a mean of 2.5 actions per hand.** Turn-based, so latency is irrelevant and a bad signal costs nothing; **the entire game is a bet, a call and a face**, which is a thumb-sized interface. ⚠️ **But the pacing target was tuned against chat being slow** (`OPEN-QUESTIONS` C5) - a phone hand is much faster, which may make race-to-5 too short. **Re-measure rather than inherit the number.** ⚠️ **Live changes what a disconnect means**, and B3 was already open: a dropped socket is not a character act, and on mobile it is not even a choice - a tunnel, a call, a locked screen. **Abandoning and leaving have to be different things, and on a phone that is a design problem rather than a detail.** ⭐ **Open, and worth deciding on purpose: live or async.** Live is a poker night. Async - a hand a day with somebody, like correspondence chess - suits a phone better, suits two people in different timezones better, and is a different game with the same rules. Docs: `SEREN/labs/squint/{HANDOFF,PRD-TRD,OPEN-QUESTIONS}.md`. | **Standalone UG game - next after the SEREN AI work** |
