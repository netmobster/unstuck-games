Your sheet says
you have Moonbeam.

It doesn't say the beam keeps burning for ten rounds without costing another action. Or that moving it costs your whole turn. Or that you're only allowed one spell slot per turn now. Most players find that out by losing a fight.

Table Sheet reads your D&D Beyond character and builds the page you actually wanted: every spell and feature in plain English, what fits in one turn, playbooks for the fights you keep having, and roleplay notes pulled from your own backstory.

The first character we ran it on had Revivify prepared and no diamond to cast it with. The second had two magic rings doing nothing, because all three attunement slots were already full. Both players had been running those sheets for months.

What you get

One page, built for a phone at a dark table halfway through a fight.

1What fits in one turn. Move, action, bonus action, reaction — and what your character can actually put in each.
2Your engine, explained properly. The one resource everything else hangs off, with real numbers.
3Fight playbooks. Opening round. One big enemy. A crowd. Someone's dying. They come for you.
4Every spell in plain words. What it does, when to use it, and when it does nothing.
5How to play them. Built from the personality, bonds and flaws you wrote yourself.

What it catches

On the way through, it checks the sheet against itself and tells you what's going to fail at the table.

Components you don't have for spells you've prepared. Armour sitting in your pack instead of on your body. Magic items you never attuned. A spell prepared twice when the class already gives it free. Resources still spent from last session. Eight concentration spells when you can only run one.

None of it gets changed for you. It's your character.

What it won't do

Republish the books. Everything on the page is rewritten in plain language, for your character, from the sheet you point it at.

Touch your sheet. It reports; you decide.

Keep your data. The character stays on your machine. Somebody else's sheet needs a token from your own browser, and it's never stored.

Install it in two lines

You need Claude Code and Node 18 or newer. Then paste a D&D Beyond character link and ask for a table sheet.

/plugin marketplace add netmobster/unstuck-games /plugin install table-sheet@unstuck-games
