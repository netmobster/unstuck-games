# GARGANTUA

*Filed 2026-09-14 by Jay: "a tamagotchi god that can't stay awake." Icon/animation: a circle that deflates on itself.*
Red-team reaction below, kept as sent. **On the board, not designed.** One Day list on the homepage.

---

OH. Gargantua.
This is actually very Unstuck.

The bit that immediately interests me isn't "Tamagotchi god." It's:

> **a god who has forgotten how to be a god.**

And the Star Wars observation is the important design insight. You don't need a dialogue tree saying Gargantua is sad. You need a tiny animated thing whose physical behavior makes you feel it.

A circle is fucking perfect for that.

## The emotional vocabulary could be microscopic

Not "animations" in the conventional game sense. State expressed through physics.

- full circle → barely holds itself together
- slight wobble → trying
- rolls toward you → interested
- stops halfway → nope
- tiny pulse → pleased
- contracts → embarrassed / tired
- slowly loses circumference → oh shit
- briefly becomes enormous → miracle
- immediately deflates again → depression wins

And then the miracles are almost insultingly basic.

Not: **CAST DIVINE FIRE**

More like: **Make flower grow.**

And Gargantua goes: … … 🌱

And you think OH MY GOD, HE DID IT.

That's the Star Wars thing you're pointing at: emotion comes from behavior, not exposition.

## The red-team concern

The phrase I'd circle in blood is: **"keep it alive"**

Because that can instantly become Tamagotchi bullshit: feed meter, sleep meter, happiness meter, daily chores, guilt notification, etc. Which would be exactly what Unstuck Games is supposed to fucking hate.

The interesting version isn't maintaining Gargantua's stats. It's:

> **Can you keep a depressed god interested in existing?**

Those are radically different games.

And "deflation" is potentially the whole mechanic. Not HP. Not hunger. **Existential buoyancy.**

You don't optimize Gargantua. You coax him.

## The god angle gives you a beautiful progression

At first, you're asking for almost nothing: *Make a flower grow.*
Then: *Make it rain.*
Then: *Make the rain stop.*
Then maybe Gargantua starts doing something you didn't ask for.

That's where I'd get very interested. Because now the player isn't unlocking abilities. They're watching a character remember itself.

And that gives you a really clean Unstuck question:

> **What if the progression system is watching someone get better?**

Not levels. Not currency. Not upgrades. Gargantua gets less deflated.

That's a fucking game hook.

And importantly, this feels like something CD could prototype very, very quickly: one circle, a handful of physical/emotional states, a tiny miracle vocabulary, deterministic state changes, and then hammer it with 20-minute observation runs.

I'd put GARGANTUA on the board. Not because it's fully designed yet. Because there's a living organ in that idea already.
