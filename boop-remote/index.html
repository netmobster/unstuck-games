    How this works

    Ask about the person. Filter on reality. Rank on fit. Explain the trade-offs.

    Matching

    Hard limits come first. If you rule out phone work, need a route open in your province, or set a firm pay floor, anything that breaks it is hidden, and the Explore page lists what was hidden and why. Everything else is ranked on interaction style, skills you enjoy, goals, schedule, pay, equipment, and how strong and fresh the evidence is. We show plain labels (“Strong fit”, “Possible fit”), not made-up percentages. Unanswered questions count as neutral.

    What “verified” means here

      A registration page doesn’t prove there’s work. A careers page doesn’t prove there’s an opening. A forum post doesn’t prove anything is current. Records marked “Kind of work to investigate” are categories, not employers.

      Staying safe

        Privacy

        Your answers and swipes are stored only in this browser’s local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere. The research prompt contains only the preferences you chose to give, and you decide where to paste it.
