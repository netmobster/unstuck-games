# Catalogue audit

Generated by `scripts/audit.mjs` from `data/catalogue.json` (built 2026-10-13). Re-run after any data change.

## Headline

- **77 records**: 60 specific routes and 17 "kind of work" category records.
- **73 can be recommended.** 2 are rejected and 2 are closed; these stay in the data so the reasons are kept, but the app never ranks them.
- **0 records claim `applications_open_confirmed`.** No official page could be opened from the research environment (see RESEARCH_METHODOLOGY.md). Every status is therefore conservative.
- **Sources:** 460 citations, of which 11 were opened directly (job postings read through a job-board connector). The rest are search-engine summaries of the cited pages and are marked `accessible: false`.
- **0 direct application links.** No sign-up page was opened, so none was published (careers-page URLs from search results are kept in each record's sources). Next steps link to official homepages.
- **Pay:** 34 records carry figures (basis: official 29, aggregator 5). 9 were published without a currency and are shown as "currency not stated", never assumed to be USD.
- **12 records are limited to a specific group**, such as youth, students, assistive-technology users or Indigenous-language speakers. They're labelled and only reach the shortlist if liked.
- **Safety guidance:** 6 official Canadian job-scam sources power the About page.

## Coverage gaps

Two research passes were made. The second (2026-10-13, two more agents) filled the biggest first-pass gaps: creative and production work, virtual assistance, appointment setting, ecommerce operations, small-business digital help, spreadsheet cleanup, and Digital Main Street. Still open:

- **Customer operations:** remote customer service at Canadian banks, telcos and airlines (no 2026 remote posting found); entry-level remote order-processing employers. TELUS, Teleperformance, Amazon, Boldly, Time Etc and Arise were looked at and dropped for thin or conflicting evidence.
- **Creative and production:** captioning employers (3Play Media's postings were US-only; Amara had no recruitment information), digital asset tagging, Dribbble, the Canada Media Fund. The production-assistant training programs found date from 2019–2023.
- **Language and writing:** content and product-description writing; a negative-signal pass for Welocalize.
- **Programs:** Youth Employment and Skills Strategy, Opportunities Fund for Persons with Disabilities, Canada Training Credit, OSAP micro-credentials; funding status for Digital Main Street and Young Canada Works in 2026–27.
- **Research participation:** Maru Voice Canada; paid study pools at other Ontario universities.
- **Geography:** Canada-first throughout. Nothing was checked for other countries, and the app says so.

Detailed per-family ledgers, including dropped candidates and open questions, are in `research/ledger_*.md`. Every automated policy change to the agents' data is listed in `research/normalization_log.md`.
