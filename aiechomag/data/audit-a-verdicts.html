1. The five claims

The research's verdict, tested

The research said none of the five held as written, none was simply wrong, and all five came back reframed. On the evidence in the research and the checks:

  • "All five reframed": no. One claim fits the red team's "reframed" (model choice), and there October's conclusion reverses September's at the level of the job. One held (accountability). One held at its core (judgment). Two split (the wrapper, being found).
  • The research's own verdict sentences say so. "Verification stayed the bottleneck" (claim 1). "The gap held" (claim 2). "The price and parity half strengthened" (claim 3). "The margin is real and now measured" (claim 4). "The channel half got stronger" (claim 5). The one-word label erased every held or strengthened part.
  • Claim 2's reframe was refuted by its own check. The research verdict said liability "began to be assigned after the fact"; check-claim-2 §2 item 5 found "none of them assigns liability yet". Without that line, October's conclusion is the 2 October roundup's own takeaway: "Accountability arrived after the fact, and from outside."
  • "None held as written" is true only word for word.
  • "None simply wrong" is true of the five bundled claims, not of the weekly statements. Three takeaways were wrong on the month's own evidence: "Judgment did not get cheap" (28 Aug to 4 Sep), whose dataset came from a nightly taste loop; "Model choice is now the least consequential choice in your stack" (5 to 11 Sep), whose lead source concluded "The same project can give me a reason to use both"; and "Faster isn't better" (26 Sep to 2 Oct), against the month's only long-run record, better, not faster.
  • The finders graded themselves. The agents that traced the claims gave the verdicts, and the editor printed them. The checkers scoped verdicts out as opinion (check-claim-1 §3), and no check covered the monthly's intro or its pattern (check-01-06-11). Rule VI, "the one who finds doesn't grade", never reached the grades.

Two passages already make the delta explicit, and are the models for the rest: 09's "The gap they were reacting to held. ... What changed in those four days is that the question was raised in public six times, after the fact." and 12's "So the defensible claim is narrower than the version The ECHO Files' roundups ran in September."
