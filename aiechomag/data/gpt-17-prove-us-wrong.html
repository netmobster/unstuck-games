How to argue with us: reply to the email this issue came in. Tell us the claim, what you know, and where we can check it. A link beats an opinion. An opinion with a reason beats silence.

What happens next: a checker that didn't write the claim tests what you send against its sources, and the result runs with a stamp, as the checked pages here do. If you're right, the correction runs in the next issue, under your name if you want it. The best arguments run whoever wins, with a reply.

We'd rather be corrected in public than be wrong in private.
