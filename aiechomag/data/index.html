The data room

Everything the issue was built from and checked against, so you can check our checking. 77 pages, every one through the gate.

How to rerun a check

  1. Pick a claim on any page of the issue. Each one carries a source tag in its section's source map, below.
  2. The tag points at a check or a research report here, and the source map gives the primary source's link.
  3. Open the source and read it against the line. The check report says what we found and why; if you find something else, prove us wrong.
