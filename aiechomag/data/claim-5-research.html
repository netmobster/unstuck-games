DATA// The five claims

Claim 5: marketing's job moved from making assets to being found (with: capability converts)

Research report, claim 5: the claim traced through September

Research agent report, 5 Oct 2026. Verbatim. Numbers and links are the agent's; CC checks every link before anything is published.

Appearances

  • 28 Aug–4 Sep (early version): judgment didn't get cheap. Tunguz: drafts 47→3, line edits flat at 136. MarTech: GEO is now a weekly job. Plan your distribution on the assumption that Google stays as powerful as it is.
  • 5–11 Sep: your corpus became a distribution channel. Evidence: Latent Space's AEO tracker (top item, 20/20), Thompson's "Write Things Down", and Ronacher's neijuan as the counter-frame. Flag: Thompson never treats models as distribution. That link was the roundup's own.
  • 12–18 Sep: money rising, map disappearing. Evidence: IAB 9.5%→12.3%, 44%, 78%→69%; MarTech's nones; Stratechery saying ChatGPT ads work. Flags:
    • IAB's 44% covers changing consumer behaviour in general, with AI search as one part.
    • 78%→69% measures focus on generative AI in media campaigns, not content-making as such. The shift is relative (76% vs 69%), not an exit.
    • Stratechery is paywalled, so ChatGPT ad performance is unconfirmed. MediaPost (9 Sep) reported CPCs from under $3 to $13 and no benchmarks.
  • 19–25 Sep: access replaced capability. Evidence: Amazon blocks Muse; IAB agentic-buying standards; martech category labels mean nothing; AI talking shoppers out of purchases.
  • 26 Sep–2 Oct: faster isn't better, and the org is the bottleneck. Evidence: MarTech's faster-not-better piece; job ads asking for process-to-outcomes; MKT1's 1,100+ posts; the AI search myths piece; Erase.com's 714 campaigns. Flags: the faster-not-better piece cites no data. The job-ads study is 60 hand-picked postings. MKT1 covers pay and equity, not AI skills.

Background

Discovery was already moving away from the click. In January–April 2026, 68.01% of US Google searches ended without one, up from 60.45% in 2024 (SparkToro, 9 Jun; the two years use different panels). ChatGPT started selling ads with a US pilot on 9 Feb (per PPC Land). Knak's 2026 survey of 333 enterprise marketers found 88% say AI content needs moderate or substantial editing, and 85% still missed a launch date.

Why it matters

If a model is your brand's first reader, the operator's job shifts from producing assets to being citable, admissible and measurable. Budgets moved first: optimising content for AI answers is buyers' top area of increased focus (76%, IAB). Measurement lags: only about 2.5% of the visits that follow an AI mention carry a referral tag (MarTech, 1 Oct).

The shift

  1. 2 Sep: the production half arrives inverted. Tunguz's five-year record shows AI lifted his weakest posts (10th percentile 2.59→3.81, graded by an AI panel) but didn't cut his time to ship. Better, not faster.
  2. 7–8 Sep: being found becomes measurable (brief 8 Sep, 20/20). Latent Space ran 7 models across 161 categories. Astra cites a median of 5 sources (Sol 9, Opus 11, Fable 15), and 28 categories have one pick every model agrees on.
  3. 10–14 Sep: the money confirms it (brief 14 Sep, 18). IAB lifted its forecast to 12.3%. AI-answer optimisation tops buyers' focus list at 76%, while focus on generative AI in campaigns slipped from 78% to 69%. 48% now measure brand visibility inside AI tools.
  4. 16–21 Sep: the channel gets paid, and talks back.
    • ChatGPT ads passed a $1B run rate around 31 Aug (OpenAI's David Dugan, via PPC Land).
    • On 16 Sep OpenAI began testing Sponsored Agents, plus tools that draft ads from your landing page. Making moves inside the channel.
    • Semrush (n=2,338, via MarTech 18 Sep): about 81% of chatbot shoppers have dropped a purchase on AI advice, but only 29% of consumers research products in chatbots. The brief (21 Sep) scored this just 11.
  5. 20–22 Sep: walls and rules (brief 22 Sep, 19). Amazon blocked Meta's Muse as an unauthorised agent. IAB Tech Lab published AAMP 3.0/OpenProposal, a standard for agent-to-agent media buying; comments close 22 Oct.
  6. 28–30 Sep: the plumbing outruns the traffic.
    • Shopify opened checkout to browser agents (TechCrunch, 28 Sep).
    • Adobe forecast that AI traffic to US retail sites will grow 130% this holiday season (29 Sep).
    • Yet Shero Commerce's 73 stores saw AI visits flat for a year while orders from AI visits rose 2.74x (September, no exact date).
    • Erase.com (30 Sep): only 37.9% of URLs cited in Google's AI answers rank in the top 10. Successful suppression campaigns published a median of 28 assets; failed ones published 29.
  7. 1–5 Oct: added to search, not replacing it.
    • Datos, via MarTech (1 Oct): AI and traditional search are growing in step. AI mentions lift brand-site visits 1.5–2.5x within a week.
    • Forrester (2 Oct): it's unclear consumers want autonomous buying.
    • SOCi (5 Oct, n>1,000): the share of consumers who used AI to find a local business rose from 9% to 52% in a year. But 83% use search, and 67% of AI users have been given wrong information.

Verdict

Reframed. The channel half got stronger in money and plumbing. But by early October, AI discovery looked like an addition to search, and it was being paid for and permissioned faster than anyone could measure it. Meanwhile the month's only long-run production record showed better, not faster.

Counter-evidence

AI isn't replacing search. Datos's State of Search Q2 2026, via MarTech (1 Oct), found AI and traditional search "growing at about the exact same rate quarter over quarter." IAB still forecasts paid search growth of 8.1% this year, and Shero's AI visits were flat for twelve months. Tunguz's numbers also run opposite to "faster, not better."

Reading list

  • The Frontier AEO Tracker: What Astra Chooses (and every other frontier model, and what you can do about it) (https://www.latent.space/p/aeo), 7 Sep 2026: primary data on which sources models actually cite.
  • IAB Raises 2026 U.S. Ad Spend Forecast to +12.3% YoY Growth, as Strong First Half Lifts Expectations (https://www.iab.com/news/iab-raises-2026-u-s-ad-spend-forecast/), 10 Sep 2026: the primary source for the month's key percentages.
  • Marketing Production in the Age of AI (https://knak.com/state-of-marketing-production/), Knak, 2026 (no day given): free, and the hardest data behind "faster, not better."
  • 7 AI search myths busted by data (https://martech.org/7-ai-search-myths-busted-by-data/), 1 Oct 2026: the corrective on AI search adding to traditional search, and on untracked referrals.
  • AI Productivity Doesn't Mean What I Thought It Means (https://tomtunguz.com/ai-productivity-doesnt-mean-what-i-thought/), 2 Sep 2026: the counter-case, with five years of receipts.
  • Meta, Amazon, And The Real Question About Agentic Commerce (https://www.forrester.com/blogs/meta-amazon-and-the-real-question-about-agentic-commerce/), 2 Oct 2026: the demand-side check on agents that buy.

Throughline

  1. Run your top 20 buyer questions through ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and Google AI Mode every week, and log who gets cited; Astra keeps about five sources.
  2. Use that log to date your AI mentions, check direct and organic lifts over the following seven days, and add an AI option to self-reported attribution.
  3. Get agent-readable: tidy your structured product data, test how browser agents handle your checkout, and get your media team's OpenProposal comments in by 22 Oct.
