3. The ledger arithmetic (The Receipts)

Ruling: the text is right but unclear. Check 2's 647 lines are 605 confirmed + 25 wrong + 12 unconfirmed + 5 overstated rows = 647. Those 5 rows held 7 overstatements. GPT added the 7 instead of the 5 and got 649. Its fallback, that the 7 are a subset of the 25 wrong, is also wrong. The sentence "This ledger counts those 5 rows as lines checked … Without them it is 642" is correct (642 + 5 = 647), but the table cell "7, in 5 rows" leads with the number that isn't added.

Clearer, two changes:

  • Table cell, Check 2, "Overstated, then corrected": "5 rows (7 overstatements)"
  • Sentence under the table: "Check 2's 647 lines are 605 confirmed, 25 wrong, 12 unconfirmed and 5 rows that overstated their source; those 5 rows held 7 overstatements."

The same problem affects Check 1 (06 O2): 196 + 39 + 1 = 236, so the 4 private lines are outside it. Cell: "4, outside the 236".
