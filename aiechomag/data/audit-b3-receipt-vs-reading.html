Audit B3: receipt against reading

Framing audit, 5 to 6 October

The AI Echo, Edition 1 · Monday 5 October 2026 · auditor B3 (CC). Red Team v3, point 2: a hard line between receipt and editorial synthesis. Report only: no draft, check or print file was edited.

Sections. 12 What does a task cost?, 13 How do you get found when a machine reads first?, 10 The month in one chart (only what prints: the page's standfirst, Proposal 1's title and its "What it shows" line), and 14 The case against (only two things: whether its factual premises are stated as receipts correctly, and whether any of its readings are presented as established fact rather than as the piece's case).

Read against. The print copy (print/edition-1-full.md; the per-section files match it), traced back through the annotated drafts, their source maps, "not tagged" lists and edit logs, edit-rulings.md, edit-notes.md, this issue's checks (checks/check-10-12-13.md, checks/check-14-15-20.md) and the pack (00-BRIEF.md, 00-RULES.md, the monthly, 03-research/ claims 3 to 5, 04-checks/check-claim-3.md to check-claim-5.md). No fact was re-checked and nothing was loaded from the web.

Levels.

  • RECEIPT: someone said, filed, published or measured it, or a checker confirmed it. It stays flat.
  • READING: the magazine's interpretation of checked facts. A line tagged only to the monthly's Verdict, Why it matters, best case against or throughline is a reading here, even under an [S: …] tag: the monthly is ECHO's own earlier reading, not an outside source.
  • HYPOTHESIS: a bet about what comes next, what it means or what will work.
  • Advice is counted apart, and flagged where it states a causal claim as settled or doesn't say what it rests on (the brief asked this of 12 and 13).

Priorities. MUST: a reader would be misled. Then SHOULD and COULD.

Where to edit. Quotes are the print text; in the drafts the same lines carry tags. Line numbers are the drafts' (drafts/, cc-sections/), where edits go before make-print.mjs is rerun. In print/ the lines run two lower for 10, 12 and 13 (the script strips their header comment) and match for 14.

Summary

48 findings: 4 MUST, 25 SHOULD, 19 COULD. One printed element checked and holding (10's chart title).

The three that matter most:

  1. 12 prints its thesis as what the evidence said (B3-12-01, B3-12-02). The standfirst states that cost per task told the labs apart, and the opening says "By October the evidence said something narrower", then gives the monthly's verdict as that evidence. 13's standfirst was already softened to "suggests"; 12's wasn't.
  2. The Receipts' ledger counts readings as confirmed claims (B3-X-01). In these four sections, 17 source-map rows are the monthly's own readings or advice, and the checks marked them CONFIRMED because they match the monthly. They sit inside "Confirmed 605".
  3. The steelman's key premise uses the monthly's word, not Cursor's (B3-14-03). 14 says Cursor cut its prompt "because newer models need fewer guardrails". The only Cursor wording in the pack is "direction", and 12 prints "less direction". The piece's step from instructions to limits rides on "guardrails".
