Who checks our checker

WHO CHECKS OUR CHECKER // 12 ERRORS PLANTED · 11 CAUGHT · THE MISS: A DROPPED HEDGE

One agent copied two features, 05 Who checks the checker? and 09 What does a task cost?, and planted 12 errors in the copies, six in each: three changed numbers, two shifted dates, two swapped attributions, two dropped hedges, a source mismatch and two drafting slips. Two fresh checkers, who weren't told anything was planted and couldn't see the originals, checked a copy each. Their reports were graded against an answer key kept outside the magazine's files. They caught all six in 05 and five of six in 09. The miss: AINews said simpler coding "may not have improved much", and the copy said it "had not improved much". The checker confirmed the line.

The caveat: each copy kept its source map, which still held the true figures, so some catches may have come from the map rather than the sources.

The verdict scale

Lines. Checkers mark every number, quote, date and attribution confirmed, wrong or unconfirmed.

Claims. Each of September's five claims gets a grade: held (substantially survives), strengthened (the evidence makes it stronger), weakened (survives with less force), reframed (the useful conclusion is genuinely different) or still open (not enough evidence). The research agents called all five reframed. An agent that didn't research them regraded them: accountability held; judgment held at its core, its first-week form weakened; model choice was reframed, reversed per job; the wrapper held on cost, weakened on quality, reframed toward thinner wrappers; being found strengthened on money and plumbing, weakened on "moved from making", still open on search. Grades are readings. No checker checked them.

What this method can't see

  • Only what the monitor reads. Thirty-seven feeds is a sample, not the field: none of the legal actions of 28 September to 1 October reached a daily brief. The scoring weights changed on 20 September, so items either side of that date weren't scored alike.
  • Paywalls. Paywalled sources were checked only as far as their public previews, and we say so where it matters.
  • One model family. Every agent that researched, checked or drafted this issue ran on Claude, and agents trained alike can miss alike. ChatGPT, as red team, shaped the structure and the cover line, and checked no facts. No second model family has checked the issue.
  • What no check covered. The grades, The Pattern's convergence and the cover line are readings. The facts under them were checked.
  • One editor. One person's questions, one person's read.
  • A closed month. The corpus closed on 5 October. Later developments wait for the next issue.
