The 441.5% and 31.3% figures are right. · Faros AI, 12 Apr 2026, https://www.faros.ai/blog/ai-acceleration-whiplash-takeaways

Correct version: "his draft churn fell from 47 to 3". · Tomasz Tunguz, 2 Sep 2026, https://tomtunguz.com/ai-productivity-doesnt-mean-what-i-thought/ (raw text: /index.md)
  • WRONG (attribution) · The Read-these note on Pragmatic Engineer: "How the labs moved humans to reviewing reviews." · The article describes humans reviewing the AI's review as the most common approach across companies in general (one example is Anthropic's Bun project, which runs several AI reviewers). What it pins on Anthropic and OpenAI specifically is triage by blast radius: AI-only review for low-risk changes and mandatory human review for high-risk ones. It adds that at Anthropic a human still merges even low-risk changes for now. The note could say something like "How review moved to reviewing the AI's review, and where the labs drop the human". · Gergely Orosz, Pragmatic Engineer, 8 Sep 2026, https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/what-is-happening-with-code-reviews
