Two caveats: the "5" is rows, while the check's own tally says 7 overstated (P14); and the unprinted CHANGELOG.md 5 October entry still says
  • Per-section stamps. The letter (21/1), Rules (8/0), Corrections (57/5) and Library (30/0) match the recount, and so do the feature stamps in the print copy (54/0, 70/2, 57/3, 58/0, 44/2, 42/2, Month 21/0). The colophon has no stamp, by D-01's "better" option.

Corrections' Added here note does correct it, but the same entry's bold headline still prints "weren't DHH's" flat (X15). The counts agree, but the Corrections entry contradicts itself.
