Audit B1: receipt against reading

Framing audit, 5 to 6 October

The AI Echo, Edition 1 · Monday 5 October 2026 · auditor B1 (CC). Red Team v3, point 2: a hard line between receipt and editorial synthesis. Report only: no draft, check or print file was edited.

Sections. 08 Who checks the checker?, 09 Who pays when an agent breaks something?, 06 The Month, 15 The Operator, 20 The Operator's Page, and 04 From the editor (short findings: Jay rewrites it).

Read against. The print copy (print/edition-1-full.md and the per-section files), traced back through the annotated drafts, their source maps and edit logs, edit-rulings.md, this issue's checks (checks/check-08-09.md, check-01-06-11.md, check-14-15-20.md, check-16-and-cc-sections.md) and the pack (00-BRIEF.md, the monthly, 03-research/ claims 1 and 2, 04-checks/check-claim-1.md and check-claim-2.md). No fact was re-checked, and nothing was loaded from the web.

Levels.

  • RECEIPT: someone said, filed, published or measured it, or a checker confirmed it. It stays flat.
  • READING: the magazine's interpretation of checked facts.
  • HYPOTHESIS: a bet about what comes next or what it means.
  • Advice (imperatives) isn't graded, unless it states a causal claim as settled.

Where to edit. Line numbers are the annotated drafts' (drafts/, cc-sections/). They are also the line numbers in the per-section print files, because make-print.mjs strips tags inside the line. Edit the drafts, then rerun make-print.mjs.

Summary

42 findings: 7 MUST, 23 SHOULD, 12 COULD.

The three that matter most:

  1. The liability bet is printed as a receipt, twice (B1-09-06, B1-15-04). "Liability is being written so that it lands on whoever deploys the agent" opens the advice in 09 and 15. It is the monthly's unchecked "Why it matters" line. 09's own intro says only one of the six actions reaches operators, and that one is a bill with no text.
  2. 08's thesis is printed as September's finding (B1-08-01, B1-08-04). The standfirst and the verdict paragraph state that the human job "moved up a level" as fact. The checkers scoped the verdict out as opinion, and its third job, gating what can't be undone, is the claim 08's own Prove us wrong box calls most likely to be wrong.
  3. The Pattern is printed as a discovery (B1-06-01, B1-04-01). The Month's standfirst and the editor's letter ("turned out to be a single move made five ways") state it as found. No check covered the pattern.

How counted: by hand, from the print copy. One sentence is one unit, and a sentence that mixes levels counts once, at the level that leads it. Headings count only where they assert something. Designer notes are left out because they repeat the body. 04 also has about 4 sentences that describe things that haven't happened yet; those belong to the checks, not this audit.

"Unmarked" is not a fault count. Most unmarked readings are plainly the writer's voice ("the plainest case") or sit under a frame that is already marked. The findings are the ones that pass for receipts. The ratio is the pattern to notice: the features are mostly receipts with readings at the joints, while The Month is mostly readings.

Cross-cutting

  1. The tag can't tell a reading from a receipt. In the drafts, a line tagged to the monthly's verdict, its "Why it matters", a throughline, #intro or #the-pattern carries the same [S: …] as a checked fact. None of those lines was checked. check-claim-1 §3 scopes out "the verdict paragraph, the interpretive sentences … and the three throughline actions", and check-01-06-11 says no check covered the monthly's intro or its pattern section. The print then strips every tag, so the reader sees no difference either. Most of the findings below are lines tagged this way. The blur started upstream: 00-BRIEF.md §8 told the drafters that 08's thesis came "from the verified record". A fix outside this audit would be to tag these lines [S-reading: …] in the drafts, so the next edit can see them.
  2. Use one marker, every time. Features say "on the magazine's reading". Departments say "our reading". The editor says "I read" or "my reading". 09 says "on our reading" in its body twice (B1-09-04). A marker readers meet often becomes one they learn to trust. One marker per paragraph is enough: a marked verdict covers the framework sentences after it in the same section. Don't stack markers, and don't put them on receipts.
  3. The How we know boxes say what was checked, never what wasn't. The brief's spec for the box is "the sources, what was checked, what's still uncertain". Neither 08's box nor 09's says that the verdict was never checked. One line in each would carry the boundary for the whole piece (B1-08-12, B1-09-09).
