1. Needs a change

WRONG and UNCONFIRMED

1. WRONG · 11 · Accountability, step 4 (row 16)

  • As written: "The FTC reportedly opened a probe into OpenAI and Anthropic."
  • What the sources say: the line matches the monthly and check-claim-2 §2 item 23, but same-day reporting contradicts it. The Next Web (30 Sep) reports three things:
    • An FTC spokesperson confirmed the probe to CNBC and CBS News on 30 Sep.
    • The spokesperson told CBS News the agency first opened it in the summer.
    • The New York Post reported it first.
  • So: the probe was confirmed, not just reported, and 30 Sep is when it became public, not when it opened.
  • Fix: "The FTC confirmed a probe into OpenAI and Anthropic that it had opened in the summer". Tag it NEW.
  • Context: this is the same finding as check-08-09 item 1. The edit notes already log the monthly's line for Corrections.
  • Source: https://thenextweb.com/news/ftc-probe-openai-anthropic-ai-labs-metr

2. WRONG · 11 · Accountability, "Ended as" (row 19)

  • As written: "these are filings, a subpoena, a reported probe, a hearing and a bill with no text"
  • What the sources say: "a probe". The agency confirmed it (item 1).
  • Why the draft has it: the line copies check-claim-2 §2 item 5, which rested on SiliconANGLE's "reportedly" account.
  • Source: as item 1.

3. WRONG (minor, scope) · 11 · How we know (row 51)

  • As written: "The timelines are the September monthly's, step for step … the monthly cut each to five"
  • What the source says: the pack's monthly is the long version. Its header reads: "This is the long version. The published post cut each 'How it shifted' timeline to three steps to fit email". A reader who opens the published monthly finds three steps per timeline and can't match the spread step for step.
  • Fix: "the long version of the September monthly (the published post carries three steps each)". The line can stand as written only if the long version is published with the issue.
  • Source: 01-the-monthly-september-2026.md, line 3.

UNCONFIRMED: none. Every tag resolved inside the pack.

Overstated (change recommended)

4. 06 · Accountability (row 6)

  • As written: "between 28 September and 1 October liability stopped being hypothetical. A state, a non-profit, a regulator, California's attorney general and two senators started naming who should answer for it"
  • What the sources say: three of the five were already acting before the window.
    • Florida: WFSU (29 Sep) reports that the injunction request was filed within Florida's original case against OpenAI, filed on 1 June.
    • The FTC: the spokesperson told CBS News the agency opened its probe in the summer (The Next Web).
    • California's attorney general: the 1 Oct release presents the subpoena as part of an ongoing investigation. It refers back to a formal investigation into the Hugging Face incident, announced the previous month.
  • What fell inside the window: new filings, a subpoena, a hearing, a bill announcement and the FTC probe going public.
  • Fix: "acted" or "went public" would hold. check-08-09 makes the same call for 09.
  • Same wording elsewhere: this is the monthly's verdict. 11's step-4 heading "liability gets names" leans the same way, but 11 qualifies it with "No liability is assigned yet".
  • Sources:
    • https://news.wfsu.org/state-news/2026-09-29/floridas-attorney-general-wants-to-stop-openai-development
    • The Next Web, as item 1
    • https://oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/part-ongoing-investigation-attorney-general-bonta-serves-investigative-subpoena

Lighter overstatements

  • 11 · "These four reached it from the same evidence" and "The spend caps trace to one post". The monthly says the four "leaned on" the same post. Model choice's cap advice also draws on Databricks' sub-budget (#3 throughline 2).
  • 11 · "it's where verification, accountability, model choice and the wrapper each ended up". These are the monthly's words, but on this spread the "Ended as" lines put only the wrapper at spend. It is the advice that ends at the cap.
  • 11 · Judgment step 2, "the most common setup elsewhere". The article describes this as the most common approach across companies in general, and its example is Anthropic's own Bun project (check-claim-1 §1 item 3 and §2). "Generally" would hold. check-08-09 flagged the same wording in 08.
  • 11 · "IAB Tech Lab's rules" (Being found's "Ended as" line and the move table).
    • OpenProposal is a proposed specification, open for comment until 22 Oct (check-claim-5 §2), as step 3 itself says.
    • Listing "Shopify's open checkout" among "limits" is the magazine's reading: it is an opening.
  • 06 · "The human step moved up a level, to defining what good looks like…". In the monthly, that phrase titles step 2 (3–9 Sep: engineers reviewing the AI's review, agents auditing the grader). The three tasks are the verdict's October end-state, and 06 fuses the two. It's a fair synthesis. check-14-15-20 item 2 flagged the same phrase in 14.
  • 06 · "the labs are taking over routing". This rests on one lab, OpenAI. AWS and Cloudflare (11, Model choice 4) are cloud platforms (check-14-15-20).
  • 06 · "the vendors converged on price: between 28 and 30 September…". The convergence came earlier: Anthropic (Sonnet 5) and OpenAI (GPT-6 Sol, from 22 Sep) were already at $2/$10 (check-claim-3 §1 item 1). The wording is the monthly's.
  • 06 and 01 · tokens and the bill.
    • 06 sets "Bills now diverge on cost per task" beside Argon's "more than twice the output tokens".
    • 01's sell line under "What does a task cost?" says "One used more than twice the tokens".
    • Both imply tokens set the bill. But output tokens are under half of each per-task figure (check-10-12-13), and at its introductory price Argon is the cheaper of the two ($1.99 against $3.26).
    • 01 should also say "output tokens" (62,000 against 27,000).
  • 01 · Cover option 2, "Prices fell." This is true of Sol, Luna and Opus 5.5 (22–26 Sep). But the $10/$50 frontier held (#3 step 2), and Sonnet 5.5 kept its list price. check-14-15-20 flagged a similar line in 14.
  • 11 · "Separate agents checked every number, quote, date and attribution: 236 lines, 39 wrong".
    • The arithmetic holds: 196 confirmed + 39 wrong + 1 unconfirmed across the five checks.
    • But four more lines, about the private briefs, were marked internal and not checked, so "every" is a shade strong.
    • Para 1 makes the same claim.
  • 11 · "drawn from 37 feeds". 00-RULES says 37 feeds "(as of mid-September)".
