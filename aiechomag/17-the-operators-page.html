The background05 · Card 01 ▾

In Anthropic's study, testers caught a swapped-in dangerous command 13.6% of the time, and about 5% after 50 or more prompts. Fewer gates, Rajesh Beri argued on 4 October, means the ones left get read.

The Hawley–Murphy AI Agent Accountability Act, announced on 1 October, had no bill number or published text as of 5 October. Watch the FTC's confirmed probe of OpenAI and Anthropic too, which the agency says it opened in the summer. Look for // who the text treats as the operator
The background06 ▾

As its sponsors describe it, the bill would make operators liable, criminally and civilly, for the "knowing operation of an AI agent that recklessly causes computer hacking damage or loss", and developers liable if they fail to build reasonable safeguards when they knew, or had reason to know, their agent could hack.
