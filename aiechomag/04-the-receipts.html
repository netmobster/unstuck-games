A line is one sentence, bullet or table row with something in it to check: a number, name, date, quote or attribution. Wrong: the source says otherwise. Open: it waits on something that hadn't happened when the issue closed, and its page names it. Every check also flagged right lines that claimed more than their source. The editors tightened them; only check 2 counted them apart. Check 2's 647 lines are 605 confirmed, 25 wrong, 12 unconfirmed and 5 rows that overstated their source; those 5 rows held 7 overstatements. Check 1's 236 lines are 196 confirmed, 39 wrong and 1 unsettled; its 4 private lines sit outside them.

Check 2's confirmed count includes our readings, which were checked only against the September monthly, not against primary sources.

The second family. On 6 October, after the close, ChatGPT (OpenAI's Go plan, model not shown, web search on) checked the issue's 19 sections: 764 rows. Two separate agents then checked its claims against the sources before any line here changed. Of its 146 unconfirmed rows, 125 were already confirmed by our checks, 14 weren't facts, 5 rest on a source behind a wall (listed under the limits) and 2 had no support in our record. Who checks our checker

WHO CHECKS OUR CHECKER // 12 ERRORS PLANTED · 11 CAUGHT · THE MISS: A DROPPED HEDGE

One agent copied two features, 05 Who checks the checker? and 09 What does a task cost?, and planted 12 errors in the copies, six in each: three changed numbers, two shifted dates, two swapped attributions, two dropped hedges, a source mismatch and two drafting slips. Two fresh checkers, who weren't told anything was planted and couldn't see the originals, checked a copy each. Their reports were graded against an answer key kept outside the magazine's files. They caught all six in 05 and five of six in 09. The miss: the line said simpler coding may not have improved much (AINews's words: "may not materially improve"), and the copy said it "had not improved much". The checker confirmed the line.

The caveat: each copy kept its source map, which still held the true figures, so some catches may have come from the map rather than the sources.

The verdict scale

Lines. Checkers mark every number, quote, date and attribution confirmed, wrong or unconfirmed.

Claims. Each of September's five claims gets a grade: held (substantially survives), strengthened (the evidence makes it stronger), weakened (survives with less force), reframed (the useful conclusion is genuinely different) or still open (not enough evidence). The research agents called all five reframed. An agent that didn't research them regraded them: accountability held; judgment held at its core, its first-week form weakened; model choice was reframed, reversed per job; the wrapper held on cost, weakened on quality, reframed toward thinner wrappers; being found strengthened on money and plumbing, weakened on "moved from making", still open on search. Grades are readings. No checker checked them.

What this method can't see

  • Only what the monitor reads. Thirty-seven feeds is a sample, not the field: none of the legal actions of 28 September to 1 October reached a daily brief (the briefs are the editor's own and not public, so this rests on his word). The scoring weights changed on 20 September, so items either side of that date weren't scored alike.
  • Paywalls and private sources. We don't go round paywalls. Paywalled sources were checked only as far as their public previews, private ones not at all, and each page says so where the claim sits. The one exception is the cover's sell line for 09, which leaves it to 09. The table below lists every claim that rests on one, and where it could fail.
  • One model family, then a second. Every agent that researched, checked or drafted this issue ran on Claude, and agents trained alike can miss alike. ChatGPT, as red team, shaped the structure and the cover line. On 6 October, after the close, it checked the issue's facts as a second model family, and its findings were checked against the sources before any line changed (the ledger has the result).
  • What no check covered. The grades, The Pattern's convergence and the cover line are readings. The facts under them were checked.
  • One editor. One person's questions, one person's read.
  • A closed month. The corpus closed on 5 October. Later developments wait for the next issue.
