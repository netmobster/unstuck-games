One agent copied two features, 05 Who checks the checker? and 09 What does a task cost?, and planted 12 errors in the copies, six in each: three changed numbers, two shifted dates, two swapped attributions, two dropped hedges, a source mismatch and two drafting slips. Two fresh checkers, who weren't told anything was planted and couldn't see the originals, checked a copy each. Their reports were graded against an answer key kept outside the magazine's files. They caught all six in 05 and five of six in 09.

The missThe line said simpler coding may not have improved much (AINews's words: "may not materially improve"), and the copy said it "had not improved much". The checker confirmed the line.
The caveatEach copy kept its source map, which still held the true figures, so some catches may have come from the map rather than the sources.

What this method can't see

Only what the monitor reads.Thirty-seven feeds is a sample, not the field: none of the legal actions of 28 September to 1 October reached a daily brief (the briefs are the editor's own and not public, so this rests on his word). The scoring weights changed on 20 September, so items either side of that date weren't scored alike.
Paywalls and private sources.We don't go round paywalls. Paywalled sources were checked only as far as their public previews, private ones not at all, and each page says so where the claim sits. The one exception is the cover's sell line for 09, which leaves it to 09. The table below lists every claim that rests on one, and where it could fail.
One model family, then a second.Every agent that researched, checked or drafted this issue ran on Claude, and agents trained alike can miss alike. ChatGPT, as red team, shaped the structure and the cover line. On 6 October, after the close, it checked the issue's facts as a second model family, and its findings were checked against the sources before any line changed (the ledger has the result).
What no check covered.The grades, The Pattern's convergence and the cover line are readings. The facts under them were checked.
One editor.One person's questions, one person's read.
A closed month.The corpus closed on 5 October. Later developments wait for the next issue.
