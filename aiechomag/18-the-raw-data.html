The Raw Data

Everything this issue was built from and checked against, published beside it, so you can check our checking.

The other seventeen pages tell you what we found. This one points at what we found it in. The data room holds the research, every check and its verdicts, the audits, the second model family's check and our ruling on each of its findings, and the feeds the month was read from. Every file went through one gate before it went in, and the gate's own page lists what it took out.

What isn't in it, and why

Other people's articlesWithheld
They appear as links, never as copies, and nothing behind a paywall is reproduced. The claims that rest on a paid or private source are listed in The Receipts, each with its failure point.
The briefs' summariesWithheld
They were written for one reader, so the briefs come in as titles, links and scores.
Our accounts and toolsWithheld
The second-family check's run log records the account it ran on and the tools that drove it, so it stays out.
Anything personal, any unreleased product, and any open security findingWithheld
The gate holds them back, and says on its page what it removed.
