The last
warren

A tower defense game where you are the thing trying not to be found. Every tick you grow and every hunter takes a step. Stay, run, hold in the trees, or spend your family to buy time.

Play in your browser · about 3 minutes a run
The question
How much stronger do I need to be before I move?
The feeling
“I should have moved one tick earlier.”
The line
Each run is a generation. A survivor carries an heirloom to the next. When a generation never sees a dawn, that was the last warren.
Tombstone
Tokyo Jungle (2012): where you settle decides what you become.
The hunters search on what they heard and saw, not on where you are. Nothing here is a score: a run ends in a sentence.
