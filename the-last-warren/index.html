The hunters · one more kind each night
Sweepers→ Trackers→ Listeners→ Hounds

They cut the ground into strips; they follow where you were; they hear through the trees; they catch runners. Two at dusk on night one, one more each night, another every twelve ticks. They share one belief about where you are and search it together. At dawn they turn for the edges.

The working

Every roll is hashed by seed, tick and who. Change one decision and only what follows from it changes, so every “found where you stayed” has a twin: the same night, leaving one tick earlier. Thirty cores play about 1,300 nights a second, and the rules aren’t done until at least a quarter of those deaths were one tick late. Any seed replays. The bot plays any policy you name.

Dawn

Keep two in five, never fewer than four. Draft one of three boons: brood · hush · teeth · walls · legs · dig deeper · dig · scatter. They start the next night where you hid.

The line

When a generation falls, one heirloom carries for each dawn it saw, three at most: a kind it was born to, or a boon it held. The hunters remember the family: their first guess is where your mothers hid. A generation that never sees a dawn ends the line.

A night takes a few minutes.
A line can survive across generations.
Every night is seeded and replayable.
