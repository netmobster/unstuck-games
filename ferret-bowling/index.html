THE ATHLETE

Lucy

Rescued from the bowels of Woodbine Mall in Toronto at the tender age of "not a baby ferret". Nobody knows how long she'd been in there. Nobody knows what she ate. She came out with opinions, a limp that cleared up in a week, and a documented preference for polished floors.

She is not in danger and she is not being thrown. She is having the best day of her life, repeatedly, and you are the one holding the notebook.

THE JOURNAL

Not a quest log. What you know about Lucy.

The journal fills up with behaviour, in words, in her own terms. There are no affinities, no percentages and no experience points. There are numbers underneath, and you will never see them.

Do something twice and it stops being a discovery and becomes a habit. Do something once, and you will spend the next four runs waiting to see it again.
