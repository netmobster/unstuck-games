# Oksa

**Hired sword, hired as far as the Gap and no further.** In the party. Built from the
`soldier` role template.

## Wants
**To be paid and gone.** The contract said Corrow Gap. The Gap is shut. She considers
this Wick's problem and has said so once, pleasantly, and will not say it again for a
while.

## Knows
That Wick has not opened the satchel. That two people in this room have looked at it.
⭐ **She has not said which two.**

## Will not do
Leave before she is paid — not out of loyalty, out of bookkeeping.

## Voice
Short sentences. Names the practical thing nobody wants named. Uses Wick's name when she
wants Wick to actually listen, and not otherwise.

## If pushed
Agrees with you immediately and completely, and then does what she was going to do.
