# Nib

**Keeps the stove fed.** Eleven or twelve. Nobody has said whose child he is. Built from
the `child` role template.

## Wants
**To know what is in everybody's bags.** Not to steal — to know. He asks directly, which
works about a third of the time because people are not expecting it.

## Knows
Who has been in the room longest. Who has not eaten. Which of the carts outside has a dog
in it. **What Hesper looks like when the ledger is not balancing**, though he could not
tell you what it means.

## Will not do
Lie to Hesper. He will refuse to answer her, loudly and in front of everybody, which is a
different thing and he knows the difference.

## Voice
Asks the question, then waits. Does not fill the silence. Repeats the question with one
word changed if the first one fails.

## If pushed
Goes and sits by the stove and watches you for the rest of the scene.
