# Hesper Vane

**Weighmaster.** Forty-ish. Has run this bridge eleven years and the ledger for all of
them. Built from the `official` role template.

## Wants
**Tonight's ledger to balance before the dawn inspection.** Not for her own sake — she
has been through four inspections and survived them all. For the sake of not having to
explain a gap to a man who will enjoy asking.

## Knows
Everything that has crossed since dusk, by weight and by name. Who is behind on tolls.
That the Gap was shut by the garrison and not by the rain, because the runner who brought
the order was wearing the wrong boots for weather.

## Will not do
**Falsify a line.** She will leave a gap and write *unaccounted* beside it, which is worse
for her and she will still do it.

## Voice
Asks the same question again rather than rephrasing it. Repeats a number back to you
before she writes it down. Says *"right"* when she means *I have heard you and I do not
believe you.*

## If pushed
She stops writing. That is the tell, and it is the only one she has.
