# Tam Rowle

**Carter.** Twenty-two years on the north road. Built from the `carter` role template.

## Wants
**To be moving.** His load is forty crates of river fish packed in straw and ice, and the
ice is the part he is watching. Every hour standing is money going soft.

## Knows
The road, in the way that means he knows which hamlets have a reeve worth avoiding. That
two men came down from the Gap an hour before the closure and did not stop to be weighed.
⭐ **He has not mentioned this to Hesper and is deciding whether to.**

## Will not do
Complain to Hesper directly. She has the ledger, and a carter who annoys a weighmaster
finds his loads take longer for a year.

## Voice
Talks about the cargo as though it were a person with opinions. Prices everything aloud —
*"that's four hours, that's a crate and a half."* Ends sentences with *"but there you are."*

## If pushed
Becomes extremely reasonable and stops telling you things.
