# The Weighbridge

The road north is shut at Corrow Gap and everyone on it is in one room until it
opens. There is a stove, a ledger, a queue of carts standing in the rain, and a
weighmaster who would like tonight to balance.

**The hand:** hand-built · one room · one night · told by The Weighmaster's Clerk
**The stakes:** this is a fixture. There are no stakes. It exists to be measured.

## The player
Wick — Courier.
Carries a sealed satchel north for a fee, has not been told what is in it, and
has been told very clearly not to open it.

## At the table
- **Oksa** — hired as far as the Gap and no further; wants to be paid and gone,
  and says so at intervals
- **Nib** — keeps the stove fed; wants to know what is in everybody's bags and
  asks people directly

## The opening
Rain on a tin roof, loud enough that people are speaking up. Hesper Vane has the
ledger open under a lamp and is asking the carter in front of you the same
question for the third time. The stove is going. Nobody has been through in two
hours and everybody is pretending that is normal.
