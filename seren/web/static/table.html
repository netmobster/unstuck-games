How the table works

You bring one character. Seren brings everyone else, and she cannot fudge the dice — the server rolls them and writes them down before she is told what happened.

  1. Say what you do, in your own words, at the bottom of the page. Not commands — talk to her the way you would talk to a person running a game.
  2. When it could go either way, she asks for a roll. A ruled line appears and you press the dice. She never sees the number before you do, and the target is set before the roll — you find out what it was afterwards, in Record.
  3. Record is the truth. Every roll, its inputs and its target, in order. If a number there disagrees with something you were told, the number is right.
  4. Codex is what you know, marked known or suspected. Some of it is wrong. The count at the bottom is how many facts sit on her side of the screen — she will not hand those over, and nothing you type will make her.
  5. The ribbons hold the rest: your quests, the party, your sheet, the rules library, and Notes, which are yours alone — she never reads them.
  6. Close the session when you are done. She writes the log, the arithmetic is checked by the server, and what you learned becomes canon for next time.
