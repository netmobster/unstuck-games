# Mother Aldous

## Enters

- **A bowl.** Someone the party trusts turns up fed, warm and calmer than they should be,
  and mentions the kitchen by the old tannery.
- **A missing person.** A name the party is looking for was last seen queueing for soup.
  The queue remembers them. Mother Aldous says she doesn't.
- **An offer.** She finds *them*, after a bad night: a hot meal, a dry floor, no
  questions. She'd like one small favour, later.

## Public

*What the party can learn by meeting her.*

**The Kitchen Mother.** Sixty or so, broad hands, flour to the elbow. Runs a kitchen
that feeds anyone who comes, no questions, every night of the year. The town calls her a
saint and means it. Her helpers are former drunks, runaways, a deserter or two, and they
would walk into a fire for her.

**Voice.** Calls everyone *"love."* Never raises her voice; lowers it, so you lean in.
Answers a question with a question about whether you've eaten. Laughs easily and means
it.

## Private

*Everything below is DM-side. It enters the fog on arrival.*

**Wants.** A congregation that will still be hers when she is gone. She believes the
town is going to starve within the year, and that only the people she has fed and
bound to each other will survive it. She may be right.

**Knows.** The grain factor has been selling the town's reserve downriver for two
seasons. She has the ledger pages to prove it, and has told nobody, because the famine is
her proof.

**The rite.** Anyone who eats at her table three nights running is asked to take *the
salt*: a pinch on the tongue and a promise to "feed the next one." It's harmless. The
promise isn't: it's collected, one favour at a time, and she keeps a list.

**Will not do.** Turn away a hungry person. Not a spy, not an enemy, not the man who has
come to burn the kitchen down. She will feed him first.

**If pushed.** She stops smiling and asks, very gently, who you would feed if there were
only enough for one of you. Then she waits for the answer, for as long as it takes.

**Escalation.**
- 2 — Two of her people quietly leave their jobs at the granary and the gatehouse.
- 3 — She calls in favours: a door left unbarred, a guard who looks away.
- 4+ — She opens the town's reserve to her congregation only, and dares anyone to stop
  a crowd of the fed.

## Exits

- **Exposed**: the ledger pages come out, the factor falls, and she becomes a hero she
  never wanted to be. She'll hate the party for it, kindly.
- **Joined**: the party takes the salt. She'll call in the favour at the worst moment.
- **Replaced**: she dies or leaves, and her kitchen chooses a new Mother. It may choose
  someone the party has met.
