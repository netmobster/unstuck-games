## The through-line: show the record, never state the moral

**Kept whys are concrete, specific, and literal** — a filed complaint, a classification, a form field, a seat,
snacks, lunch, a straight line, a door, "purpose: yes", "extremely Alberta", Fern. The theme leaks out of an
image or a bureaucratic record. The funniest and saddest ones are both *reports*.

"becoming louder" 15, "storage given away" 2).

## Rules for batch 2 (added to CONTENT-SPEC founding rules)

1. **One concrete image or one bureaucratic record per why.** A form, a field, a classification, an object, an action.
2. **Never state the theme.** No aphorisms, no "proof that", no "the kind of X that Y". If it could go on a mug, cut it.
3. **Prefer the Prestige's paperwork or her literal log as the voice** — both kept categories.
4. **Tender works when it's small and specific** (a seat, a door, "right back"), not when it's a summary.
