# Game #2 — the shape so far

One page, synthesised from [BOUNDARIES.md](BOUNDARIES.md) after seven decision rounds. Not a design doc:
it's what the boundaries add up to, for Jay to react to before step 3 (testing). Untitled.

## In one breath

The Prestige is auditing a glitch. **Jame** fixes things and leaves. While he's gone, **Not Betsy**, a
deranged, oversized escape pod who takes every word literally, chases his goal, salvages, picks up
strays and bolts on sci-fi tech nobody would ever fit to her. He comes back to her log, in her voice, and a
ship that is **always different: not always better, always weirder**. Three stops. At the end nobody wins
or loses. **You leave a why.**

## The loop

**Present (Jame):** land, read her log, deal with the consequences, fix the planet's problem with whatever
absurd parts she has now, set a goal, draw three last words and say one, leave.

**Away (Not Betsy):** a real-time week per stop (fast-forward allowed; staying away pays a little, never
enough to force it). She reads Jame's goal and last words like a job description, rolls encounters,
salvages scrap into mutations, collects strays, and reacts to Prestige fronts moving on their own clocks.

**At a stop:** the planet has a problem, customs intake happens, or both (RNG). Her mutations are useless
right up until they aren't.

**Each stop obsoletes the last:** Not Betsy mutates per stop, so every epoch is a new ship with new
superpowers and new liabilities. Every positive comes with a negative, and the reverse. Not balanced: absurd.

## Stakes without win/lose

- **Scrap** builds, **Power** bounds how far she wanders, **Smudge** is how impossible she is to optimize.
- The Prestige pushes back; the UI goes from terminal-clean toward rust and breakage at the edges.
- If she's archived, **loss carries forward**: the Synthesis keeps one mutation and "improves" it for the next Not Betsy.
- The bargain ends in **one sentence of why**, built from what she became. The Archive keeps it.

## Money
Free is the whole game. **God Mode: keep one mutation** past the end of a stop. One ad per game per day, then repeatable one-hour activations (see `GOD-MODE.md`).

## Kept from Universal Paperclips on purpose
Each stop obsoletes the last · permission < power · mechanics are the theme · end on the first verb.

## Lifted from our library
Real-time absence + orders into a moved world (Elsewhere) · fronts and clocks, knowledge ledger (SEREN) ·
deterministic sim, sweeps, causality probe, God Mode = intervention, homepage is the game (Orbis).

## What this game contributes
**Corpus → a world the player plays inside**, and a candidate second entry: **an ending that is a sentence,
not a score.**

## Constraints
Web, no runtime AI (every literal reading and every why is authored and deterministic) · Bad Monkeys canon ·
MVP is the full first bargain · UI built first, then designed by CD with SVG sprite layers.

## What step 3 has to prove
1. **Always weirder:** returns are different in interesting ways, never broken-different or boring-different (a divergence sweep, not a growth curve).
2. **A week away is a story, not a cleanup job:** Elsewhere's absentee test, adapted.
3. **The why lands:** generated sentences read as meaning, not Mad Libs, across thousands of runs.
4. **Last words bite:** each of the three lines visibly changes what she does (causality probe).
5. **Mutations meet planets:** absurd parts solve problems often enough to feel like fate, rarely enough to feel earned.
